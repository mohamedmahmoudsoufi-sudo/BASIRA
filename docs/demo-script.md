# BASIRA demo script — 2 minutes

## 1. Frame the problem — 15 seconds

Open BASIRA on the default synthetic case. Say: “A scam alert is useful only if the analyst can explain why. BASIRA connects the link, the indicators, and the possible next step without opening the destination.”

## 2. Submit the suspicious URL — 25 seconds

Point to the URL field and keep the preloaded synthetic URL visible:

`https://emiratesnbd-secure-check.xyz/verify/account?session=48291&continue=login`

Click **Analyze safely**. Call out that the URL is parsed locally. Highlight the high-risk destination, score, and the four weighted signals: higher-risk domain ending, unusual hostname structure, possible Emirates NBD impersonation, and action-oriented path.

## 3. Explain the evidence — 25 seconds

Open **Evidence explorer**. Show the submitted URL, effective domain, and signal artifacts. Emphasize the labels: Evidence is what the string contains; Inference is what the signals may indicate; Hypothesis is a reviewable possibility, not a confirmed event.

## 4. Reconstruct the journey — 30 seconds

Open **Scam journey**. Walk through: URL submitted, structural signals extracted, possible impersonation path, and likely next step. Point to the confidence bars and say: “BASIRA does not invent a click or a login. It marks uncertain steps as hypotheses so a human can review them.”

## 5. Close with protection — 25 seconds

Open **Recommendations**. Show the order: do not interact, verify via the official app or manually typed official domain, report, and recover safely if credentials were already exposed. Finish with: “The value is not just detecting the scam. It is making the safer next decision explainable.”

## Backup interaction

Click **Load demo case** to restore the synthetic URL. Toggle **عربي** to show RTL labels. Do not paste a real password, OTP, card number, or private message during the demo.
